# Cut Labz frequently asked questions

## What is Cut Labz?
Cut Labz is a membership-based barbershop in Manchester city centre. Instead of paying for each haircut, members pay a monthly fee for unlimited haircuts and book appointments when it suits them.

## How does a Cut Labz membership work?
Pick a plan (Core, Plus or Premium), join, then book appointments online. Membership cuts are free at checkout while your membership is active. Memberships are billed monthly in advance, and they are for personal use only.

## What are the three membership plans?
Core is £29.99 per month for haircuts only. Plus is £39.99 per month and adds beard trims. Premium is £69.99 per month and adds beard trims and free add-ons. All three include unlimited haircuts.

## What is the difference between Plus and Premium?
Both Plus and Premium include unlimited haircuts and beard trims. Premium also includes the add-ons for free, such as the hot towel treatment, hair wash and dry, face mask or steam, and hairline filler spray. On Plus the add-ons cost £5 each. Plus is £39.99 per month and Premium is £69.99 per month.

## What is the difference between Core and Plus?
Core is £29.99 per month and covers haircuts only. Plus is £39.99 per month and also includes beard trims.

## Are there unlimited haircuts, and is there a limit?
All memberships include unlimited haircuts. To keep appointments fair, a member can hold only one upcoming membership cut at a time, and cannot book a date after their membership period ends.

## Do I have to book, or can I walk in?
Cut Labz does not run a walk-in queue. All appointments must be booked in advance online and are subject to availability.

## How do I join a membership right now?
Create an account, then go to the Membership page and choose a plan. At the moment online payments are not set up, so joining starts a free 30-day trial membership. This may change when payments are introduced.

## Can I try Cut Labz without a membership?
Yes. You can book a one-off Haircut for £20 or a one-off Haircut + Beard for £25, with no membership needed.

## Are memberships refundable?
No. All memberships are non-refundable and Cut Labz does not give partial refunds. You can cancel at any time and the membership stays active until the end of the billing cycle.

## Can I share my membership with someone else?
No. Memberships are for personal use only.

## What happens if a payment fails?
Under the Cut Labz terms, failed payments may result in suspension of services and the cancellation of bookings.

## What does an appointment cost if it is not covered by my plan?
Anything outside your plan is charged at the normal price. For example, add-ons on a Core or Plus plan are £5 each, and a one-off Haircut is £20.

## Where can I read the full terms?
The full Terms & Conditions are on the Cut Labz website at /terms.
