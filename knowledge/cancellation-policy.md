# Cut Labz cancellation, reschedule and no-show policy

## How much notice do I need to cancel or reschedule?
Cut Labz requires a minimum of 24 hours' notice to cancel or reschedule an appointment. You can cancel or reschedule yourself online from your account page until 24 hours before the appointment starts.

## What if my appointment is less than 24 hours away?
Cancelling or rescheduling within 24 hours is not available online. Please contact the shop directly on +44 7913 784603 or at contact@cutlabz.co.uk. Under the Cut Labz terms, a late cancellation incurs a £5 fee.

## What is the late cancellation fee?
A late cancellation, meaning less than 24 hours' notice, incurs a £5 fee under the Cut Labz terms and conditions.

## What happens if I do not turn up?
A no-show incurs a £5 fee. Repeated no-shows may result in restricted access to booking.

## What if I arrive late?
Clients must arrive on time. A late arrival may result in a reduced service time, or the appointment may have to be rescheduled.

## How do I reschedule an appointment online?
Sign in, open your account page and choose Reschedule on the appointment. Pick a new date, time and barber, then confirm. Your cut and price stay the same. If you are a member, the new date cannot be after your membership ends.

## How do I cancel an appointment online?
Sign in, open your account page and choose Cancel on the appointment, then confirm. Once cancelled, the time becomes available to other customers, and you can book a new appointment straight away.

## Can I cancel my membership?
Yes. A membership can be cancelled at any time and stays active until the end of the billing cycle. Cut Labz does not give partial refunds, and memberships are non-refundable.
