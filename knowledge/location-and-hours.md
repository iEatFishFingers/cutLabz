# Cut Labz location, opening hours and contact

## Where is Cut Labz?
Cut Labz is a membership barbershop in Manchester city centre. The address is Cut Labz, Phenix Salon Suites, 1 Deansgate, Manchester, M3 1AZ. It is about 2 minutes from Manchester Arndale.

## What are the opening hours?
Cut Labz is open Tuesday to Saturday, from 11:00 to 21:00. Cut Labz is closed on Sunday and Monday. Appointments are only available during opening hours, and individual barbers can have time off on particular days.

## Is there parking near Cut Labz?
There are multiple secure paid car parks within a 2 to 3 minute walk of Cut Labz. They are easy to reach from Deansgate and the surrounding streets. Cut Labz does not have its own car park.

## Who are the barbers at Cut Labz?
The barbers at Cut Labz are Wayne Graham and Ayaan Ali. When you book online you can choose a specific barber, or choose "Any available" and Cut Labz will assign whichever barber is free at your time.
