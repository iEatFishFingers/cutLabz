# Cut Labz one-off prices and add-ons

## How much is a one-off haircut?
A one-off Haircut at Cut Labz costs £20. A one-off Haircut + Beard costs £25. One-off appointments are for people who are not members, or who want to try Cut Labz before joining a membership.

## Which add-ons are available and how much are they?
Cut Labz offers four add-ons, each priced at £5:
- Hot Towel Treatment: £5
- Hair Wash & Dry: £5
- Face Mask / Steam: £5
- Hairline Filler Spray: £5

Add-ons do not make an appointment longer. They are chosen when you book.

## Are add-ons free for members?
Add-ons are included for free on the Premium membership. On Core and Plus memberships, add-ons are available at £5 each. Customers who are not members pay £5 per add-on too.

## Is membership cheaper than paying per cut?
A one-off haircut is £20, while Core membership with unlimited haircuts is £29.99 per month. Core becomes cheaper than paying per cut once you have more than one haircut a month. Plus is £39.99 per month and Premium is £69.99 per month.

## Should I try a one-off before joining a membership?
Yes, if you want. Cut Labz offers one-off appointments so you can experience the shop first. Many clients switch to a membership after their first visit.
