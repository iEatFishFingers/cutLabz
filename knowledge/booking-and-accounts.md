# How booking and accounts work at Cut Labz

## Do I need an account to book?
Yes. You need a free Cut Labz account to book an appointment. You create one by signing up with your name, phone number, email and a password. Having an account also lets you see, cancel and reschedule your own appointments online.

## How do I book an appointment?
Go to the Book page and follow the steps: choose your cut, choose your barber (or "Any available"), pick a date and time, then check your details and confirm. You will see a confirmation with a booking reference that starts with CL-. Cut Labz has no walk-in queue, so every appointment is booked in advance.

## How long is an appointment?
Every cut is booked as a 30-minute appointment, and add-ons do not make the appointment longer.

## How far ahead can I book?
You can book up to 28 days ahead, starting from today. Appointments must be booked at least 60 minutes before the start time, so a time that is less than an hour away cannot be booked. Start times are offered every 15 minutes during opening hours.

## Can I choose my barber?
Yes. Choose Wayne Graham or Ayaan Ali, or choose "Any available". With "Any available", Cut Labz assigns the barber who is free at that time.

## How many appointments can I hold at once?
You can hold up to 3 upcoming bookings at the same time. Members can hold only 1 upcoming membership cut at a time. Once your upcoming cut is completed or cancelled you can book the next one.

## How do members book a free cut?
A member books the same way as everyone else. If you have an active membership, the membership cut for your plan is free at checkout and the total shows £0. You can only book the membership cut that matches your own plan, so a Core member books the Core Membership Cut. Members cannot book a date after their membership ends, and those dates are greyed out in the date picker.

## What if I am not a member?
You can still book a one-off appointment at its normal price. Membership cuts are only for members. If you try to book one without a membership, the site points you to the membership page to join.

## Can I choose add-ons when I book?
Yes. You can add extras such as a hot towel treatment, hair wash and dry, face mask or steam, or a hairline filler spray when you choose your cut. Each add-on is £5, except on the Premium plan where add-ons are free.

## How do I manage my appointments?
Sign in and open your account page. It shows your upcoming appointments and your past ones. From there you can reschedule or cancel an upcoming appointment, as long as it is at least 24 hours away.
